Privacy notice

This notice describes the current static fan site at The Dreamer’s Den. It is an informational summary, not legal advice. Review and update it if the site’s features or applicable laws change.

Information you provide

This site does not currently operate an email newsletter, account system, checkout, or server-side contact form. The public feedback form prepares a draft issue on GitHub; if you submit it, the content is published and handled under GitHub’s privacy statement. Do not put private or sensitive details in public feedback.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as request, browser, and network data in providing and securing its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices and retention information.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics is disabled by default because no measurement ID is configured. If the site owner configures one, analytics scripts load only after a visitor chooses “Allow analytics.” A choice is saved in this browser’s local storage under dreamers-den-analytics-consent; this site does not use that storage for advertising. Choosing “Reject” keeps analytics disabled. Clear the site’s browser storage to reset the choice.

When enabled and accepted, Google may process usage data under its own terms and policies. The site owner must review regional consent and disclosure requirements before enabling analytics.

External links

Links to reading platforms, libraries, GitHub, and other third parties are governed by those services’ own privacy practices. This site does not control them.

Contact

Use the contact page. Feedback submitted there is public on GitHub. A private email or private contact system has not been configured.
